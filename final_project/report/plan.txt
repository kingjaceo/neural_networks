Key observations the report must explain
----------------------------------------
- Training loss is the unbounded Wasserstein critic loss; large negative magnitudes
  and high variance are expected and not by themselves a sign of failure.
- Visual quality varies sharply across presets:
    * preset_02, preset_06: recognizable land/water structure, plausible blob shapes.
    * preset_03, preset_04: large water regions with small land features; visible mode
      collapse (e.g. preset_04 keeps producing a single bright island in dark water).
    * preset_01, preset_05, preset_07: dominant-color background with one collapsed
      blob; the discrete 6-tier terrain palette is not being recovered, only smoothed
      gradients.
- Probable causes of palette failure: (i) CPU-only training limited us to 50 epochs;
  (ii) the discrete piecewise-constant palette is non-trivial for a tanh-output conv
  generator to reproduce -- it has to learn sharp thresholds rather than smooth ramps;
  (iii) BatchNorm in G under WGAN-GP is a known mild instability source.


Architecture (as run)
---------------------
Generator (4.83M params):
  z(100) -> Dense(8*8*512) -> Reshape(8,8,512) -> BN -> LReLU(0.2)
        -> 4x [UpSample2x + Conv2D(3x3) + BN + LReLU(0.2)]   filters: 256, 128, 64, 3
        -> tanh -> 128x128x3

Discriminator / critic (2.79M params):
  128x128x3 -> 4x [Conv2D(4x4, stride 2) + LReLU(0.2)]       filters: 64, 128, 256, 512
            -> Flatten -> Dense(1)
  No normalization (BatchNorm is incompatible with per-sample gradient penalty).

Loss: Wasserstein + gradient penalty (lambda = 10), n_disc_steps = 5 D updates per G
update. Adam(lr = 2e-4, beta_1 = 0.5, beta_2 = 0.9).

Conditional variant adds a 7-dim one-hot:
  G: concatenate to z before the first Dense.
  D: concatenate to the flattened conv features before the final Dense.
